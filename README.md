# Mindful City Explorer

We're participating in Warsaw Open Data hackathon.  We're thinking about doing an app that hepls with mental health and also helps to expore the city.  It will give you ideas of events that you can attend or stuff to do (like explore the park, explore jyms, pools etc.) You have to fill an application with questions about you, your goal, later the algorithm will choose for you  ideas what you can attend, than you need to accept it or decline and choose what tasks will you do (take 3 pictures, reflect with voicemessage, talk to a stranger). Than the application will save user experience and work with this data. The user can access his past pictures, can build his expirience and the profile will describe what interests do you have. After the activity you can quickly give a review (choose if it was positive or negative expirience, do you want to explore more similar events). Than it can show you like gallery stories how was your month etc. It will help person to notice how diverse his life is, help to be active city member and actually find his community. I'm thinking about achitecture, how can the main menu look. It will be a phone application, the user also can choose if he's interested in adult or family events etc. Maybe also add a map so you can see if it's in your neighbourhood or you need to go to the city center. Give me prototypes and maybe hints if you have ideas how to improve the concept.
